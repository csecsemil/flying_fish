What is a Peregrine Falcon?

The Peregrine Falcon is widespread bird of prey know for its incredible speed during its characteristic hunting dive, called a stoop

Facts:

  • Top speed: Over 320km/h (200mph) in a dive.
  • Diet: Mostly medium-size birds like pigeons and ducks.
  • Habitat: Found on every continent exept Antarctica.
